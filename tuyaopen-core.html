From connectivity to interaction and media.

Integrate device connectivity, cloud AI interaction and media transport services according to product requirements.

01

IoT

Device and cloud connectivity

Cloud connectivity organized around activation, data point interaction and updates, with optional LAN and Bluetooth services.

AI Agent
