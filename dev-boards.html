Tuya T5AI

Tuya T5AI platform — dual-core ARM Cortex-M33 with on-chip KWS, voice activity detection, and lightweight AI inference, plus Wi-Fi 6 / BLE 5.4 connectivity. Rich peripheral interfaces for any IoT scenario, purpose-built for low-power edge-cloud multimodal LLM interaction across voice, video, audio, and GUI.

Tuya

T5-E1 Wi-Fi & Bluetooth chip module. Two packages: T5-E1 (onboard antenna) and T5-E1-IPEX (IPEX connector). Start from scratch with default platform overlay.

Tuya T5AI-Board

Tuya

Tuya T5AI-Board is a voice and screen multi-interaction development board based on the T5-E1-IPEX module, which is an embedded Wi-Fi and Bluetooth combo module developed by Tuya. The board is equipped with 2 microphones and 1 speaker, supporting voice recognition and playback to enable voice interaction functionality. Through the I/O connectors on the board, you can use an LCD display stack module to implement touchscreen and camera capture capabilities. You can also design your own LCD screen, supporting multiple interfaces including I2C, SPI, 8080, and RGB.

WiFiBluetooth LEAIAudioconfigurable

Tuya T5AI-Core

Tuya

Tuya T5AI-Core Development Kit is a highly integrated voice core development board based on the T5-E1 module. The T5-E1 module, independently developed by Tuya Smart, incorporates embedded Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities, making it suitable for a variety of smart hardware scenarios. This board features an onboard microphone and speaker, supporting local voice recognition and audio playback to meet the requirements of voice interaction applications. The board is equipped with a 44-pin header, making it easy for you to quickly develop and prototype AIoT projects. An onboard lithium battery power management circuit supports low-power design, suitable for developing mobile and portable smart devices. This board provides extensive hardware interfaces and comprehensive voice functionality, so it stands out as an ideal choice for you to develop AIoT voice interaction applications.

Tuya T5AI Pocket

Tuya

Tuya-T5-Pocket is a portable handheld device built on the open-source TuyaOpen framework. It supports deep integration with multimodal AI Agent and large language models, as well as audio-video multimodal large models. Powered by Tuya T5 Wi-Fi & Bluetooth chip module, the project features fully open-source hardware design and software code. It comes with abundant onboard sensors and powerful external expansion compatibility. This compact T5 AI board integrates mono display screen, camera, AXP2101 power management unit, BMI270 IMU, joystick and cellular module.
