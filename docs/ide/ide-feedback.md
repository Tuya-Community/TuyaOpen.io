Use the feedback entry in TuyaOpen IDE to submit diagnostic information when you encounter a problem.

1. Click the speech-bubble feedback icon in the lower-left corner of the IDE.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions and complete the form. The required diagnostic information is collected automatically.
3. Submit the feedback.
